Help

Report a civic issue

Sign in with a citizen account, choose Report, add an optional title and description, select the issue location on the map (or enter coordinates if map tiles are unavailable), choose a category, and submit. A ticket ID is shown after it is saved.

Find and track a ticket

Choose Track and enter the ticket ID. Ticket lookup does not require sign-in. The public view shows the ticket timeline and limited complaint details.

Review progress and resolution

Use the ticket timeline to see recorded progress. When an officer submits work for citizen confirmation, the owning citizen can review the private evidence and confirm the resolution or reject it to reopen the issue.

Department officers

Officers sign in with an account issued by the platform. Their work queue is limited to their department. They can start work, upload resolution evidence, and submit completed work for the citizen to review.

Privacy

Account and complaint information

Accounts store a name, email address, password hash, role, and (for staff) department association. Reports can store a title, description, category, ticket ID, workflow history, account owner, deadlines, and a location pin or coordinates.

Photos and evidence

A citizen’s selected report photo is preview-only in this interface and is not uploaded. Department resolution evidence images are uploaded to private local storage by default, or to configured private S3-compatible storage, and are served through an authorization-checked endpoint.

Public visibility

Ticket lookup for visitors provides a limited summary and omits complaint descriptions, precise location, account identifiers, and event notes. The public issue map displays eligible tickets with approximate, rounded locations and safe ticket fields. Owners and authorized department staff can see details permitted by their role.

Authentication and access

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sign-in uses a signed, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie. Citizen access is limited to their own reports; officer access is department-scoped; administrators have the permissions defined by the application.

The application does not define a retention schedule in its interface. Database and uploaded evidence retention depend on how the deployment is operated and configured.
